# Pre-formal review and acceptance

The sampler alone changes training exposure. Per-class draw totals and loss weights
stay fixed; repeated buildings are intentional and audited. Event totals were already
nearly balanced, so total-event balancing was rejected in the prespecified plan.
No equal-loss/brightness combination is tested. The original baseline source and
cache modules remain byte-identical. A is rebuilt under the new trainer, with a
secondary historical trajectory audit only after the complete matched contrast.

Synthetic CUDA demo: 2 tests passed, 418.642 seconds. Four A/B x B1/B2 combinations
compare continuous against paused/resumed training exactly; export and pair guards
pass. Evidence: verification.json and local output/event_sampling_demo_acceptance.log.
Queue syntax and CLI checked; real metadata preflight passed before launch.
Self-review only, no independent review. No scientific result claimed at this stage.

Limitations: with-replacement reweighting changes unique exposure; preserving step
count does not preserve information per epoch. Within-event effects cannot establish
unseen-event transfer or identify all causes of geographic performance differences.
